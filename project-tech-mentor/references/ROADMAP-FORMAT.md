# ROADMAP.md Format

`ROADMAP.md` is the route from the mission to independent delivery. It records capabilities and evidence, not a list of tutorials.

## Template

```md
# {Technology} Roadmap

## Route

| Order | Project | Capability added | Scope boundary | Evidence | Transfer check | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | {name} | {one capability} | {what is deliberately excluded} | {proof} | {changed requirement} | pending |

## Current stage
{The project and capability currently being practiced.}

## Adaptation notes
{Why the order or difficulty changed in response to diagnostic or project evidence.}

## Capstone
{The goal, constraints, and independent evidence expected when the route is complete.}
```

## Rules

- Start with four to eight projects and refine the later entries as evidence accumulates.
- Each project earns its place by adding one observable capability or a meaningful transfer constraint.
- Keep one source of difficulty changing at a time: scope, ambiguity, interacting concepts, or scaffold removal.
- Mark a project complete only after its evidence and learner explanation are recorded.
- A capstone gives a goal and constraints; it does not give tutorial steps.
